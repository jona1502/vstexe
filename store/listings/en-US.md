# InputRack

## Short description

Use your VST3 effects on your microphone, locally and in every app.

## Description

InputRack routes your physical microphone through your installed 64-bit VST3
effects. Build a chain with EQ, compression, de-essing, noise suppression or
other plug-ins, then send the processed signal through a virtual audio cable to
Discord, OBS and other applications.

Audio processing stays entirely on your PC. No audio or usage data is sent to
InputRack.

Included for free:

- VST3 scanning with a crash-isolated plug-in scanner
- Effect ordering and bypass
- Rack preset import and export
- Input/output meters and clipping indication
- Guided virtual-cable setup
- Start with Windows through Windows settings

InputRack Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that adds named profiles, automatic
profiles per application and global hotkeys. Try all Pro features free for 14
days first. No subscription.

Use in other applications requires a separately installed virtual audio cable
such as VB-CABLE. InputRack does not include an audio driver or VST3 effects.
